# Signal: Surprise Rate

## Description

The share of Analyses that recorded at least one Surprise: an unanticipated outcome, good or bad.

## Metric

Analyses with at least one bullet under Surprises that does not begin with "none" (case-insensitive), divided by all non-Bypassed Analyses; also Surprises per run.

## Purpose

Tracks how completely Predictions anticipate side effects. A falling rate with steady Accurate and Helpful shares means better anticipation; a rising rate means Predictions are missing consequences.

## Notes

No values yet; `praxis-flow-learn` writes the first value under a Values heading when a run is recorded.
