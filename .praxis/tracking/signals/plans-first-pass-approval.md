# Signal: First-Pass Plan Approval

## Description

The share of revision chains whose first Plan was Approved without a revision (no `Revise:` note), at Predict or Discern.

## Metric

Chains whose first Plan was not revised, divided by all chains, per run and across runs; a chain starts at a Plan whose Notes cite no revised Plan.

## Purpose

Whether Discern is a real gate: too low means Plans are written carelessly; always 100% means Discern may not be finding what the Analyses later find. Read with Result Consistency and the Surprises.

## Notes

No values yet; `praxis-flow-learn` writes the first value under a Values heading when a run is recorded.
