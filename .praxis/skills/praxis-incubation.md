# Skill: Praxis  Incubation

The process for creating skills and patterns, incubating confidence, promoting out of incubation, and demoting back into incubation on low confidence.

## Origin
Initial Implementation

## Purpose
Incubate potential new Skills. 

## When to Use
When new sequences of Patterns are identified as helpful or beneficial

## How it Works

## Requirements
A unique Pattern

## Inputs
Patterns and Pattern components

## Procedure

Create a new skill under `.praxis/incubator/skills/` using `.praxis/templates/skill-incubation-template.md`

## Output

Either a new skill, tool, or script  under `.praxis/incubator/`, or a new Pattern in `.praxis/tracking/patterns.md` and `.praxis/tracking/pattern-details.md`

## Success Criteria

Either a new skill, tool, or script  under `.praxis/incubator/`, or a new Pattern in `.praxis/tracking/patterns.md` and `.praxis/tracking/pattern-details.md`
