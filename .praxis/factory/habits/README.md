# Habits

Specific repeated or repeatable sequences of deterministic actions: each habit is an ordered list of scripts, hooks, tools or other habits (Rule: Every Habit Step MUST Name a Factory Product Other Than a Skill or Agent), at Structural or below. Skills name habits in their steps.

## Habit Lifecycle

Habits follow the factory lifecycle defined at `.praxis/lifecycles/factory-lifecycle.md`.

## Habit Template

Each habit is created in `.praxis/factory/habits/${habit}/`, with a `README.md` from `.praxis/templates/habit-template.md`.

## List of Habits

A list of habits can be found at `.praxis/factory/habits.md`, with more information available at `.praxis/factory/habits/${habit}/README.md`.
