# Confidence

Current confidence levels for commands, tools, skills, patterns, and beliefs.

## Levels

**None** — no confidence in successful outcomes without harmful or detrimental outcomes. Repeated harmful or detrimental outcomes after repeated revision. Do not use.

**Low** — used fewer than 3 times, or has produced detrimental or harmful outcomes. Treat results with caution.

**Medium** — used successfully across multiple cycles without harmful outcomes. Occasional failures noted but pattern is reliable.

**High** — consistent correct results across many cycles without any harmful or detrimental outcomes. Suitable for use without extra verification.

**Complete** — exhaustively verified with no known failure modes. Behavior is fully predictable. Reserved for items with extensive, consistent track records. Fewer confidence updates required.
