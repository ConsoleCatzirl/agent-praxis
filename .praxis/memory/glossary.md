# Glossary

## Prompt

The block of text recieved by the agent from the user.
A Prompt may contain multiple distinct Actions.

## Response

The block of text sent by the agent to the user.

## Conversation

A chronological series of Prompts and Responses.

## Outcome

### Beneficial Outcome

An Outcome that aligns with one or more Domains and is neither Detrimental nor Harmful.

### Detrimental Outcome

An Outcome that violates an expectation or has side-effects that modify state outside of the Praxis Loop.

### Harmful Outcome

An Outcome that violates a need or principle, or is directly opposed to a value or goal.

### Neutral Outcome

Any Outcome that is neither beneficial, detrimental, nor harmful.

## Domain

One of:
 - needs
 - principles
 - expectations
 - values
 - goals
 - beliefs
