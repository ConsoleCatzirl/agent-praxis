# Outcome Lifecycle

This document defines the lifecycle stages for plans tracked in `.praxis/state/outcomes.md`.
A Plan is a list of Tasks to perform during Act

## Stages

- Actualized
- Analyzed

## Stage Flow

Actualized -> Analyzed

## Stage 1: Actualized

The outcome has been actualized.

**Entry Paths:**
- A plan is performed and the outcomes are recorded

**Exit Paths:**
Analyzed:
- The outcomes are analyzed

## Stage 2: Analyzed

The outcome has been analyzed.

**Entry Paths:**
- An analysis for this outcome is recorded
