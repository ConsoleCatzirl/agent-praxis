# Rule: Every Habit Step MUST Name a Factory Product Other Than a Skill or Agent

Rule created: 2026-11-24T17:18:59-08:00
Rule modified: 2026-11-24T17:18:59-08:00

Status: Proposed

Confidence: Low

## Description

Every step of a habit's Procedure MUST name one script, hook, tool or habit: its type and path, or for a habit its name. A step MUST NOT name a skill, an agent or a Pattern, and MUST NOT be prose. A habit MUST NOT include itself, directly or through other habits. A habit's level is the highest of its steps' and MUST NOT be above Structural.

## Protection

A habit is a specific repeated or repeatable sequence of deterministic actions; naming only deterministic products keeps it that way, so a skill can rely on a habit without judging its result.

The habit template (`.praxis/templates/habit-template.md`) has a Procedure of numbered steps by product type, and a skill that creates or refines a habit checks each step against this Rule.

## Violation

A habit step that names a skill, an agent or a Pattern; a prose step; a step naming a product that does not exist; a habit that reaches itself; a habit at Qualitative.

## Exceptions

None.

## Conditions for Revision

Habits that need judgement keep being wanted (then they are skills), or another product type is allowed in a habit.
