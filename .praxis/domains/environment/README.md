# Environmental Conditions

## Environmental Condition Lifecycle

Environmental conditions follow the evidence-based lifecycle defined at `.praxis/lifecycles/evidenced-lifecycle.md`.

## Environmental Condition Template

Each environmental condition is created at `.praxis/domains/environment/${item-name}.md` from `.praxis/templates/environment-template.md`.

## List of Environmental Conditions

A list of environmental conditions can be found at `.praxis/domains/environment.md`, with links to more information for each at `.praxis/domains/environment/${item-name}.md`.
